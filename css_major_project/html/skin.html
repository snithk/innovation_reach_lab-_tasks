How to Pick the Right Peel for Your Skin Type: The Ultimate Guide That Actually Works

Admin Showness  |  September 4, 2025

Choosing the right chemical peel can transform your skin, but with so many options available, how do you know which one is right for you? Whether you're dealing with acne, pigmentation, dullness, uneven texture, or signs of aging, the perfect peel depends on your unique skin type, concerns, and goals. This comprehensive guide will help you make an informed decision and achieve the radiant, healthy skin you deserve.

EXPLORE PEEL TYPES BOOK A CONSULTATION

Types of Chemical Peels

Every skin concern has a peel built for it — here's how the most popular ones compare.

Glycolic Acid Peel

An AHA that exfoliates deeply, smoothing fine lines and brightening dull skin.

Best For: Normal to oily skin, dullness & uneven texture

Salicylic Acid Peel

An oil-soluble BHA that dives into pores to clear congestion and calm breakouts.

Best For: Acne-prone & oily skin

Lactic Acid Peel

A gentle, hydrating AHA that exfoliates without stripping the skin's moisture barrier.

Best For: Sensitive & dry skin

TCA Peel

A medium-depth peel that targets deeper pigmentation and stubborn acne scars.

Best For: Pigmentation & deeper scarring

Mandelic Acid Peel

A large-molecule AHA that exfoliates slowly and gently — ideal for melanin-rich skin.

Best For: Sensitive & melanin-rich skin

Yellow Peel

A retinoic-acid peel that works gradually over several days for anti-aging results.

Best For: Anti-aging & pigmentation
